        HOW TO PLAY

        Find the right standard

        1. Remember. Read three names in three seconds.
        2. Explore. Tap a box and use its contents sheet to find the codewords. Pinch or use + to zoom.
        3. Collect. Open the tray and select three bottles. Tap any bottle on your bench to replace it.
        4. Beat the clock. You have 60 seconds and one extra look at your notes. Fill your bench, then check your selections. Answers also appear when time runs out.
